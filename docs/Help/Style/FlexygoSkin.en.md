# Flexygo 2026 Skin <span class="fh-version-tag" title="Available since version 10.0">10.0+</span> {.fh-title-with-image}

![Flexygo 2026 Skin](../../docs_assets/images/FlexygoSkin/skin-2026.png){ .fh-image-of-title }

The Flexygo 2026 skin refreshes the look of the whole application with side navigation, a cleaner layout for pages and modules, and a new typeface. You can choose the light theme, the dark theme, or the theme that follows your device’s appearance.

## Where to find it
The skin applies to the whole application and is configured in the profile interface. The side navigation groups the menus and can be collapsed. At the bottom, you’ll find the user menu, with access to your profile and alerts. The theme option is in this menu.

## How to use it
1. Use the side navigation to open a page. Menu groups expand in place.
2. Open the user menu at the bottom of the side navigation.
3. Choose the light theme, the dark theme, or the theme that follows your device’s appearance. The application changes immediately and remembers your choice for your user.

## Settings
In the profile interface, set **Skin: Flexygo 2026**.
