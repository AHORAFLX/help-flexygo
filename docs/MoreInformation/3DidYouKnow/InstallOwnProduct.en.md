# Did you know the Flexygo installer also installs your own product?

Besides Flexygo, SAT and CRM, the installer installs any product built with Flexygo from its NuGet packages. Choose **Custom**, type your packages' namespace and enter your NuGet server or a folder with the packages. The installer finds them all and shows the versions they have in common; pick one and install it the way you prefer: IIS or Docker.
