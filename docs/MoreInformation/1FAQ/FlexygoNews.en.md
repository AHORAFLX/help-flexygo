# Where can I find news about Flexygo?

There are two main channels for keeping up to date with Flexygo:

## Official website

At [www.flexygo.com](https://www.flexygo.com) you'll find all the relevant information about the latest developments, news and events, as well as details about training courses and new products available in the marketplace.

## YouTube channel

You can subscribe to the [official YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UC-7BjatZYomkTcYsGkcr8YA) to stay up to date through videos.
