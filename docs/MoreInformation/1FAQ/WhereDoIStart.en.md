# Where do I start?

If you've already installed Flexygo, take a look at this playlist, which shows you step by step how to get started with your first Flexygo projects.

You can find this short online course on [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLNXy4eLtYnza8BJWQ-iB5hLAs2-VVpxPP).

If you'd rather, you can also take the course on [Udemy](https://www.udemy.com/aprende-flexygo/).

And don't hesitate to contact us if you have any questions or issues.
